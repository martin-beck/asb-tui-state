# AR-1337 — Final performance results and report presentation

## Scope

Add the completed-run results route. Present final performance results,
measure-level values and statuses, failures, provenance, artifacts, and
comparison against selected recent runs. Keep live, simulated, replayed, and
unavailable evidence visibly distinct.

## Dependencies

Depends on AR-1336 and AR-1223.

## Acceptance

- Completed runs expose deterministic newest-first history with bounded search,
  selection, and comparison of compatible reports.
- Results show each requested measure, missing/failed measures, units,
  uncertainty where available, and a clear next action for failures.
- Incompatible provider/model/catalog/configuration generations are reported as
  conflicts and are never ranked as if comparable.
- The user can return to the pool/configuration route and rerun or reconfigure
  without losing the prior report.
- Tests cover successful, partial, failed, cancelled, replayed, and conflicting
  reports, including privacy-safe output and no secret/path disclosure.
