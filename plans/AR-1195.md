# AR-1195: Cross-repository wire compatibility

Acceptance criteria:

- checked-in ASB v1.4 and v1.5 fixtures parse through the standalone adapter without translation
  ambiguity;
- catalog and lifecycle envelopes, names, numeric generations, target metadata, digests,
  provenance and closed reason/state enums match ASB exactly;
- catalog digest is recomputed and verified, and runner/operation/idempotency bindings are retained;
- malformed, unknown, stale, unsigned, mismatched and unsupported-version responses fail closed;
- exact-head interoperability fixtures and CI run against the declared ASB PR/release pins;
- no ASB renderer/Ratatui code is introduced and no vendored files are patched;
- signed DCO review, local/full tests, hosted CI and durable state evidence are complete.
