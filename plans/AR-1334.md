# AR-1334 — Benchmark pool and measure selection route

## Scope

Add the user-facing benchmark-pool route after setup. Users can browse/search
supported benchmark groups, select all or individual benchmarks, and
sub-select or deselect measures before review. The route must preserve choices
across filtering and resize and must explain unavailable or incompatible
choices.

## Dependencies

Depends on AR-1326 and AR-1333.

## Acceptance

- Workloads, groups, benchmarks, and measures are sourced from a validated,
  generation-bound catalog and have stable identifiers.
- Group selection, individual toggles, tri-state states, search, and reset are
  deterministic; filtered actions never mutate hidden selections.
- Empty, duplicate, unavailable, incompatible, and oversized selections fail
  closed with one actionable recovery choice.
- The formal UI model, help catalog, UI module inventory, and UI_OWNERS remain
  synchronized and CI checks the bindings.
- A renderer-neutral test proves a selected pool can be handed to a campaign
  without silently adding or dropping a measure.
