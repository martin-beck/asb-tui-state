# AR-1332 reconciled development control-runtime fixture

## Outcome

Provide a deterministic disposable control-runtime fixture that can qualify
AR-1323 without requiring an authorized production runtime. The fixture must
exercise the real provisioning handoff and helper contract while starting from
and recovering to a reconciled control catalog.

## Scope

- private, fresh state-root/bootstrap contract for development qualification;
- startup reconciliation of control-catalog mutations and stale locks;
- digest-only `auth_enroll` followed by negotiated v1.10 `auth_helper_invoke`;
- successful typed `AuthStatus` projection plus typed reconciliation-required,
  restart, retry, and idempotency cases;
- exact recording of runtime revision, endpoint identities, helper executable
  digest, logical locator, and result digests without recording raw credentials;
- an asb-tui-compatible fixture/harness and focused CI evidence. Production
  authorization, provider reachability, and production key management are not
  prerequisites for this development AR.

## Acceptance

- a clean disposable run provisions a broker channel and negotiates v1.10;
- enrollment commits, the allowlisted helper executes, and the subsequent
  helper mutation returns a successful digest-only `AuthStatus`;
- stale `Intent`/`NeedsReconciliation` state is repaired deterministically,
  while a genuinely uncertain mutation remains fail-closed and retry-safe;
- restart/reconnect and repeated idempotency-key cases produce no duplicate
  credential mutation and never expose helper stdout or a raw secret;
- the fixture records exact revision/endpoint/helper identities and explicitly
  labels the evidence development-only and unauthorized;
- AR-1323 can be resumed only after this evidence and the asb-tui exact-main,
  formal-model, privacy, and hosted gates are green.

## Cross-repository note

The runtime-side catalog/reconciliation implementation belongs to the ASB
control runtime. The asb-tui repository owns only the client-compatible fixture
harness, typed projection, and qualification evidence; no ASB source change is
authorized by this AR.
