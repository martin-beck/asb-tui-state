# AR-1326 — Guided benchmark and comparison route

## Scope

Add one TUI route after setup for workload/agent selection, benchmark launch,
optional response recording, strict offline replay, and result comparison.
Display evidence provenance and prevent mixed provider/model comparisons.

## Dependencies

Depends on AR-1222, AR-1223, AR-1325, and development provider integration AR-1329.

## Acceptance

- Users choose supported workloads and agents from catalog lists and confirm a
  campaign before launch.
- Capture/replay state is explicit; offline mode never calls a provider or
  silently falls back to live execution.
- Reports distinguish live, simulated, replayed, unavailable, and unsupported
  evidence and provide a selectable next action for failures.

The development prototype qualification is owned by AR-1330. This AR does not require a live
router, provider authorization, or production helper; those are separately labeled future work.
