# AR-1325 — First-class setup wizard route

## Scope

Bind the existing wizard model to the shared ASB setup contract so first-run
and reconfiguration are one route. Render selectable agents, providers, auth
methods and provider-supported models; support apply-to-all/default and
per-agent overrides; and surface credential-helper status without raw secrets.

## Dependencies

Depends on AR-1192, AR-1197, AR-1317, AR-1321, AR-1323, and AR-1324.

## Acceptance

- Every screen is selection-driven with typed validation and a visible summary
  before apply.
- Add-provider, edit, reset, apply-to-all, and per-agent operations are
  generation-fenced and recoverable after cancellation or restart.
- The TUI consumes the ASB catalog digest and never invents models or auth
  methods.
