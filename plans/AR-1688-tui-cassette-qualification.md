# AR-1688 — TUI runner-owned cassette capture and replay qualification

Qualify the TUI recording-campaign and strict offline replay screens against
the ASB runner-owned lifecycle. Cover selected and all workload choices,
redaction/sealing, incomplete coverage, exact compatibility, and network
denial.

## Acceptance

- Selected/all workload recording uses the runner-owned campaign and publishes
  only complete sealed coverage.
- TUI reports duplicate, mismatched, corrupt, or incomplete captures without
  partial offline readiness.
- Strict offline replay consumes the exact cassette without provider contact.
- Human output is self-explanatory and structured output contains no secrets.
- Exact-head receipt, hosted checks, independent review, and privacy scan pass.
