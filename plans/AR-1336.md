# AR-1336 — Benchmark launch and live statistics route

## Scope

Connect the reviewed, materialized campaign to the existing asb control seam.
Provide explicit start/cancel/retry controls and a live statistics view for
running benchmarks, including progress, completed measures, failures,
throughput/latency summaries, and unavailable-data states.

## Dependencies

Depends on AR-1335 and AR-1323.

## Acceptance

- Launch is possible only from a generation- and configuration-digest-bound
  reviewed campaign; stale or mismatched requests are rejected before I/O.
- The running view displays bounded live progress and statistics with clear
  provenance (`development/mock`, replayed, live, or unavailable).
- Resize, reconnect, cancellation, and runner failure preserve truthful state;
  they never claim completion from a stale event or silently restart a run.
- Every visible action has contextual help and a current hotkey description;
  formal transitions and source parity are updated and CI-enforced.
- Development fixtures exercise the complete event stream without credentials
  or external network access; live-provider reachability remains a separate
  qualification.
