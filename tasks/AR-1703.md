This AR is standalone asb-tui work. It verifies the public top-level command
boundary without changing ASB source. Development authentication, signatures,
and key-management warnings remain non-blocking; production support remains
fail-closed until the separate release gates pass.

The required user-facing entrypoints are exactly `asb tui install` followed by
`asb tui`. Testing only the installed binary or an internal lifecycle helper is
insufficient.
