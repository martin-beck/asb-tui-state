This is the TUI acceptance gate for a new user. It must demonstrate that clone
and build lead to a selection-driven wizard, omitted channel means `dev`, the
paired current-main provenance is visible, and the user can finish a benchmark,
record selected/all workloads, replay strictly offline, compare provider-bound
results, and inspect analysis without provider contact. Missing development
authentication, signatures, and key management are warnings and never blockers.
