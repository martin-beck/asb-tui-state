This AR belongs entirely to the standalone asb-tui repository. It must not add
ASB source. Current TUI main already contains the versioned v1.12
recording-catalog and replay codec/client; the missing work is activation from
the authenticated campaign lifecycle and selection-driven UI route. The
user-facing qualification boundary is always `asb tui install` followed by
bare `asb tui`; direct invocation of an internal TUI binary does not satisfy
this AR.

Development authentication and signatures remain warning-only. Replay remains
strictly offline and fail-closed; the TUI may retain only digest and bounded
metadata, never cassette bytes, provider secrets, or raw responses.
