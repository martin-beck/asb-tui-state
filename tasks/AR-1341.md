Qualify a disposable fresh-user journey from `asb tui install` (defaulting to
`dev`) through current-main resolution, temporary clone/build, atomic install,
`asb tui` launch, status, upgrade, removal, and offline/network failure. Bind
all evidence to exact ASB and asb-tui commits and classify the result as
development-only. Include no API keys, private paths, prompts, or transcripts.

This AR does not qualify stable release installation or production
authentication; those remain future channel-specific work.
