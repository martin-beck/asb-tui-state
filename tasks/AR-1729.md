AR-1703 independent qualification reproduced a startup race twice: a valid,
very fast noninteractive `asb tui install` child exited after `posix_spawn` but
before `tools/run-operator-quickstart.py` completed process-identity and
`tcgetpgrp` observation.  The runner then reported `PTY child is not its
controlling foreground group` before it could drain and classify the command
result.  The retained exact AR-1703 receipt and focused interactive PTY tests
remain valid; this AR owns the follow-up harness repair.

Only the asb-tui qualification runner, its focused tests, and directly related
documentation are in scope.  Do not change the application renderer, wizard,
ASB launcher, provider behavior, or production policy.  Interactive launches
must continue to prove a live authenticated session leader, controlling PTY,
foreground process group, nonzero window, bounded readiness/quit, and teardown.
Development authentication, signing, and key-management warnings are
non-blocking, but process identity, cleanup, tests, review, and CI are required.
