The coordinator must record spec reference, revision, evidence class, evidence
reference, digest, and pass status through a supported mutation. Malformed,
stale, mismatched, or unowned acceptance attempts must be rejected without
metadata mutation. Development-only authentication, signatures, and key
management remain visible warnings and never block this prototype contract;
production authorization remains fail-closed.
